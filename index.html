How the app works

From sign-up to trading: a transparent, step-by-step system that takes less than five minutes.

01 Create your account

Real name, email, phone; selfie identity verification and 2FA setup. One-time €1 access fee.

02 Pedal to earn

The app measures your distance with GPS in the background. Every 20 km is automatically credited to your balance as 1 BTCYC.

03 Collect the bonuses

Extra BTCYC from city QR boards, Bike Fest and contests; quests and badges earn you XP.

04 Trade & spend

Turn your savings into real products in the market, transfer to a friend with 2FA, or spend them in the rental shop.

Frequently asked

Frequently asked questions

What is BTCYC — is it real money?
BTCYC is the virtual reward unit you earn by riding. It is traded in the market for real products (1 BTCYC ≈ 0.80 €) and can be transferred between wallets with 2FA approval. It is not a currency you can withdraw to a bank account; its purpose is to reward the sport.
How is my distance measured?
The app tracks your rides with GPS in the background; distance, duration and average speed are recorded. Every 20 km automatically credits 1 BTCYC to your balance. Each completed ride is archived on the map and appears in the “Recent activity” ledger.
Why is there a 40 km/h cap, and what does the 100 km daily limit mean?
The platform encourages safe riding: sections above 40 km/h don't count toward earnings and go on your violation record; after three violations the account is temporarily suspended (you can appeal in the panel). The daily 100 km limit exists to prevent earning abuse — the excess is still logged, but no credit is produced.
How secure is my account?
Sign-up requires a real name, email, phone and selfie verification; 2FA is requested again for every sensitive action (trades, transfers, security settings). Sessions are listed with device and location info, and you can close a suspicious session with one tap.
What are Bike Fest and the contests?
Bike Fest is a meetup held twice a year (May and September); it grants a participation badge and bonus BTCYC. Contests refresh every month: medals go to the top three in the most distance, most QR and social leaderboards, and the leader earns a crown badge.
Is there a registration fee?
You need to pay a one-time €1 fee for access (no real payment is taken in the demo). Once you're registered, 1 BTCYC is loaded as a gift; after that all your earnings come from riding.
